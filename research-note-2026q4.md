## Covered-call overlay note

The earliest listed expiration across all four names is **2026-10-23** (weeklies are available for AAPL, MSFT, NVDA and AMZN alike). All four names also carry standard monthlies beginning 2026-10-30 and LEAPS out to January 2029, so the overlay can be rolled at weekly or monthly cadence without gaps.

## Cross-portfolio observations

- **Scale:** AMZN posts the largest revenue base ($716.92B FY2025) but the lowest net margin of the four; NVDA posts the highest net margin by a wide margin (FY2026 net income of $120.07B on $215.94B revenue).
- **Balance sheet posture:** NVDA is the most conservatively financed (total liabilities of $49.51B against $206.80B of assets), while AAPL carries the highest liabilities-to-assets ratio, consistent with its buyback program and negative retained earnings.
- **Sector concentration:** three of four names are Technology; AMZN (Consumer Cyclical) is the only diversifier in the sleeve.
